Our services, in detail

Four services, taken separately or together, quoted in writing before anything is ordered.

Program 01 · Layout first

Space planning

We measure properly and plan the layout around daily use: where people enter, sit, cook, work and store things. Furniture is specified to fit the plan rather than the plan being bent around furniture someone already bought.

  • Per room or project
  • Homes and offices
  • Quoted in writing
  • Access to measure

Program 02 · Chosen in your light

Finishes & materials

Samples behave differently in a showroom and in your north-facing room at four in the afternoon. We build a coordinated palette, order physical samples, and look at them in the actual space before anything is committed.

  • Per project
  • Any space
  • Quoted in writing
  • Samples delivered

Program 03 · Specified, not guessed

Furnishing & styling

Pieces specified to the measured plan, with lighting planned as a layer rather than a single ceiling fixture. We work to a stated budget and tell you where spending more genuinely shows and where it doesn't.

  • Per room or project
  • Any space
  • Quoted in writing
  • An agreed budget

Program 04 · Trades on the same page

Project coordination

A design only survives installation if everyone works from the same specification. We hold the schedule, confirm lead times before orders are placed, and coordinate deliveries and trades so the painter isn't waiting on a floor that arrives three weeks late.

  • Renovations
